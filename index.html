    Why the School Marquee Needs Big Text (and No QR Codes)

    Our outdoor LED marquee is 256 pixels wide by 96 pixels tall — about 1/10th the width of a phone screen.

    • Small text turns into illegible blocks: Letters shorter than 12–14 pixels tall only have a handful of LED lights to form curves like "e", "a", and "s".
    • QR codes don't scan outdoors: A QR code needs dozens of tiny crisp squares plus white borders. On 96 vertical LEDs with outdoor sunlight glare, phones cannot focus on it from a car.
    • Keep it under 10 words: Families drive past in 2–3 seconds. Big bold text with high contrast reads cleanly from the street.
